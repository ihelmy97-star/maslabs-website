Privacy Architecture

Zero-Knowledge Security by Design

At MAS Labs, we believe your medical records belong exclusively to you. MediFamily employs mathematical client-side cryptographic guarantees so that no third party, cloud provider, or advertiser can ever access your family's health data.

  • ✓
    On-Device Encryption: All health logs, doctor visits, and photos remain in your phone's private sandbox vault.
  • ✓
    PBKDF2 SHA-256 Salt Derivation: Backup encryption keys are derived from your verified identity salt, preventing dictionary attacks.
  • ✓
    16-Character Emergency Recovery Key: Master envelope protection ensures you never lose access to your medical history.
  • ✓
    Zero Commercial Ad Trackers: No third-party advertising networks or data brokers. Anonymous performance telemetry is 100% unlinked from personal health records.
🔐

Hardware-Enclave Security

Verification credentials and cryptographic salts are stored in hardware-level enclaves (Apple Keychain & Android KeyStore).
