Zero-Cloud Architecture

Why Central Health Clouds Are Risky

Cloud medical vaults create concentrated targets for ransomware and third-party data broker profiling. MediFamily stores your health records where they are safest: in your own hands.

✘ Cloud Health Apps & Portals
  • • Central Honey-Pots: Millions of patient records stored in centralized cloud databases attract sophisticated cyberattacks.
  • • Data Brokering Risks: Many consumer health apps share de-identified health metrics with advertisers and analytics firms.
  • • Account Lockout Traps: If you forget your password or an authentication service goes down, you cannot view your lab results.
✔ The MediFamily Vault Standard
  • • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Files are encrypted locally using AES-256 with keys managed by your device's native secure enclave.
  • • Zero External Transmission: Photos and documents never leave your sandboxed local storage without your direct action.
  • • Instant Offline Access: Access critical bloodwork, allergy histories, and imaging summaries even in airplane mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Document Security

How are my documents and health records encrypted?

All attached document photos, lab scans, and clinical notes are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption within your device's sandboxed local storage. Encryption keys are generated and protected on your physical hardware without ever touching external servers.

Can MAS Labs or third parties see my lab reports or medical records?

No. MediFamily operates on a strict zero-knowledge architecture. MAS Labs does not possess access keys, user databases, or central servers capable of decrypting or reading your personal health records.

What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Under our Zero-Hostage Data Policy (Vault Mode), your existing profiles, medication histories, document photos, and vital logs remain 100% accessible, readable, searchable, and exportable forever. We never lock or delete your personal health history.

Can I export all my records into a single PDF for my doctor?

Yes. MediFamily features a 1-tap consolidated PDF generator that compiles active medications, vital trend tables, allergy lists, and immunization histories into a clean, printable clinical summary ready for doctor visits.

How many photos can I attach per medical record?

You can attach up to 10 high-resolution photos per record entry, making it easy to capture multi-page hospital discharge summaries, multi-panel blood test results, radiology reports, or prescription labels.
