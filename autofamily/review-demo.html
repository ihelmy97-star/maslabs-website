Planned Unedited Walkthrough Chapter Flow

Continuous cold-launch verification sequence aligned with Guideline 2.1 and Guideline 5.1:

Chapter 1

Cold Launch With Zero Login and Instant Offline Garage

Launch AutoFamily from the iOS home screen on a clean install. Confirm the app loads the branded splash screen and enters vehicle onboarding or My Garage immediately with zero sign-up wall, zero login prompt, zero email input, and zero network dependency.

Chapter 2

Create Vehicle Profile, Tyre Specs, and Digital Cockpit

Open Add Vehicle, pick a popular make or enter a custom make, choose a paint color swatch or dropdown selection, enter model, year, license plate, and odometer reading, and configure Standard Tyre Specs alongside Compatible Tyre Specs. Save the vehicle, verify its card shows the Primary and Road Ready badges cleanly in My Garage, and tap Cockpit to enter the Digital Cockpit.

Chapter 3

Dual-Trigger Maintenance Schedule and Spare Parts FEFO Deduction

Navigate to Maintenance, create a dual-trigger maintenance interval using both mileage and calendar months, and add an item to the Spare Parts Shelf such as an engine oil jug or oil filter with expiration date and stock count. Tap Log Service, select the linked spare part, save the completed service entry, and show the automatic stock deduction and updated maintenance countdown.

Chapter 4

Fuel Log, Glovebox Document Upload, and PDF Passport Export

Open Fuel & Telemetry, record a refueling entry, and verify running-cost calculations update on screen. Open Glovebox, add a sample registration or insurance document, and trigger Maintenance Passport PDF generation to demonstrate local PDF preview and iOS share sheet export.

Chapter 5

Offline Encrypted Backup and Single-Vehicle Transfer

Open Backup & Security, set an on-device Protection Password, capture the generated 16-character Emergency Recovery Key, and run an encrypted .autofamily full backup without any email verification prompt. Demonstrate exporting a single-vehicle .autofamilyprofile package protected with a local transfer password.

Chapter 6

StoreKit 2 Pro Paywall and Restore Purchases Flow

Open the Upgrade to AutoFamily Pro paywall screen from Settings or a Free Exploration banner. Show transparent pricing for autofamily_pro_monthly, autofamily_pro_annual, and autofamily_pro_lifetime, tap the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links, and tap Restore Purchases to verify native StoreKit restoration feedback without errors.

Chapter 7

Local Vehicle Deletion and Vault Cleanup

Return to My Garage, open a vehicle card menu, tap Remove Vehicle, and confirm deletion to prove 1-tap local vehicle removal. Open Backup & Security or Saved Reports to delete a locally created archive or PDF, demonstrating full user data deletion control.
