Privacy Architecture

Built Offline From Day One

AutoFamily keeps sensitive household vehicle records entirely under your control on local storage.

Local-Only Vehicle Vault

AutoFamily launches directly into My Garage with zero mandatory sign-in. Vehicle profiles, service logs, odometer entries, fuel expenses, and scanned receipts stay inside the private application sandbox on your phone.

Encrypted Portable Backups

Full vault archives in .autofamily format and single-vehicle handoff files in .autofamilyprofile format are encrypted client-side inside background worker isolates using AES-256-CBC encryption with 10,000-iteration PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation.

Instant On-Device Deletion

Because MAS Labs operates zero central account servers, removing a vehicle inside My Garage or uninstalling the application deletes local records immediately. Review our Data Deletion Guide.

Core Capabilities

Complete Offline Vehicle & Document Records

Organized maintenance tracking, receipt capture, quota transparency, and printable vehicle history reports.

Receipts, Quotes & Spare Part Photos Everywhere

Attach cropped photos or PDFs to Service Logs, Fuel & EV Charging receipts, Workshop quotes/business cards, Spare Parts, and multi-page Glovebox records with live slot and MB quota indicators.

Glovebox Quota Transparency & Batch Share

Capture multi-page scans or import PDFs for registrations, insurance policies, inspection sheets, FEFO spare parts, and logbook notes with live per-record file and MB headroom counters, full-resolution swipeable zoom, multi-select batch sharing, and offline PDF merging.

Certified Maintenance Passport with Invoice Appendix

Export 1-tap offline PDF Maintenance Passports that summarize service history, schedules, and telemetry while automatically appending scanned service invoices, fuel receipts, and merged PDF documents.
