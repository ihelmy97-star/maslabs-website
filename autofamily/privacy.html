AutoFamily Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Version: 1.0 • Publisher: MAS Labs

Core Privacy Guarantee: AutoFamily is engineered as a 100% offline, zero-knowledge vehicle maintenance and family records vault. Your vehicle details, service logs, odometer readings, fuel expenses, registration scans, insurance papers, and workshop notes reside strictly on your personal device and are NEVER transmitted to or stored on central cloud servers.

1. Information We DO NOT Collect

AutoFamily enforces strict data minimization and decentralized local storage. MAS Labs does NOT collect, read, sell, rent, or harvest:

  • Vehicle & Ownership Records: Vehicle Identification Numbers, license plate numbers, insurance policy PDFs, registration scans, service invoices, maintenance schedules, and odometer histories remain strictly inside your device sandbox.
  • Document Photos & Receipt Scans: Workshop bills, part photos, inspection certificates, and warranty attachments stay locally in your private encrypted application container.
  • Real-Time Location & Telematics: AutoFamily does not collect continuous GPS routes, telematics telemetry, driving behavior, or location history.
  • Device Screen Lock & Biometrics: Physical access protection relies strictly on your operating system passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or Android screen lock. AutoFamily does not display in-app biometric prompts and MAS Labs never accesses biometric templates or device passcodes.
  • Commercial Advertising Trackers: AutoFamily contains zero third-party ad networks and zero cross-app behavioral tracking profiles.

2. Offline Operation & Zero Cloud User Accounts

AutoFamily v1.0 operates completely without cloud user registration or account logins:

  • No Account Required: You never create an online username, password, or server profile to manage your vehicles or household fleet.
  • Non-Personal Version & Campaign Check: AutoFamily performs a read-only HTTPS GET request to https://maslabs.app/api/autofamily/version.json to verify public app release numbers and promotional campaign status. This request sends zero personal identifiers, VINs, license plates, vehicle data, location coordinates, or advertising device IDs.
  • Privacy-Preserving Stability & Store Commerce: Platform app stores handle subscription validation directly on device. If optional diagnostic telemetry is enabled on your platform, it excludes personal vehicle details, document text, and attachments.
  • Voluntary Support Communications: If you email support@maslabs.app for help, your email address and inquiry text are processed solely to answer your support request.

3. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Backup Architecture

When you export an encrypted full vault archive in .autofamily format or single-vehicle handoff package in .autofamilyprofile format:

  • All archive payloads are encrypted client-side inside background worker isolates using AES-256-CBC encryption with PKCS7 padding prior to leaving the application container.
  • Encryption keys are derived mathematically using 10,000-iteration PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key stretching paired with unique 128-bit cryptographic random salt headers bound to your Protection Password or 16-character Emergency Recovery Key across both .autofamily backups and .autofamilyprofile vehicle handoff packages.
  • MAS Labs never receives, escrows, or stores your backup passwords or recovery keys and cannot decrypt exported files.

4. Device Permissions & Strict Functional Scope

AutoFamily requests only minimal operating system permissions when explicitly needed by user-initiated workflows:

  • Camera: Used solely when you tap to capture a photo of a service receipt, registration card, inspection sheet, or vehicle badge into local storage.
  • Photo Library & Document Picker: Used strictly to import existing vehicle invoices, registration images, or PDF records that you select.
  • Notifications & Scheduled Maintenance Reminders: Used strictly to deliver local user-configured service due alerts, inspection renewals, and insurance expiry reminders on time.
  • Boot Rescheduling & Temporary Wake Lock: Allows pending local maintenance reminders to re-register after device restart and keeps encryption uninterrupted while actively exporting or restoring .autofamily vaults.

5. Data Retention, Portability & On-Device Deletion

Because MAS Labs stores zero customer vehicle databases on remote servers, you hold complete authority over deletion and portability:

  • Single Vehicle Removal: Open My Garage, tap the overflow menu on any vehicle card, select Remove Vehicle, and confirm deletion. You can also tap Export Vehicle First inside that dialog before deleting.
  • Batch Vehicle Removal: Open My Garage, activate multi-select mode, pick the vehicles to remove, and confirm Delete Selected Vehicles.
  • Individual Records, Reports & Archive Cleanup: Delete individual maintenance schedules, service logs, fuel entries, workshops, spare parts, logbook notes, or glovebox documents on their module screens. Delete generated PDFs inside Saved Reports, delete local .autofamily or .autofamilyprofile archives inside Backup & Security, and remove leftover unlinked cache files via Optimize Storage inside Storage & Diagnostics.
  • Full Local Sandbox Erasure: Uninstall AutoFamily on iOS, or clear App Storage and uninstall AutoFamily on Android, to permanently erase autofamily.db, attachment folders, local backup archives, and app preferences from your device sandbox.
  • No Server Account to Delete: Because MAS Labs creates zero cloud user accounts and stores zero customer vehicle databases on remote servers, zero residual user profile data exists on external servers after local removal.
  • Portable User Exports: You may export printable PDF Maintenance Passports or encrypted .autofamily archives under your control at any time.

6. Compliance With Store & Regional Privacy Standards

This Privacy Policy aligns with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines, Google Play Developer Program User Data Policies, and the General Data Protection Regulation.
