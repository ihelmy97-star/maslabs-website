Architecture & Regulatory Overview (Guideline 2.1 Compliance)

Summary of architectural facts answering Apple App Review's standard onboarding questionnaire:

🔒 Zero-Knowledge & Serverless

MediFamily requires no account creation, no login, and no passwords. All health records, medications, and vitals are stored strictly in the local sandboxed device file system with AES-256 encryption.

🗑️ Account Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

Because user identity is not stored on remote servers, account deletion is achieved via 1-tap Local Profile Deletion. Deleting a profile completely and permanently purges all associated records with zero cloud trace.

🚫 Zero User-Generated Content (UGC)

MediFamily is a private household utility. There are no social feeds, public forums, user-to-user messaging, or public sharing features. Therefore, UGC reporting and blocking mechanisms (Guideline 1.2) do not apply.

💳 100% Native Apple StoreKit 2

All monetization is handled natively through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit 2). MediFamily offers Monthly ($3.99), Annual ($34.99), and Lifetime ($69.99) tiers, with full "Restore Purchases" support.

🩺 Non-SaMD Health Organizer

MediFamily is an informational scheduling and wellness tool. It is NOT a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) under FDA, EU MDR, or UK MHRA regulations, and does not provide clinical diagnoses or treatment advice.

🌍 100% Global Feature Parity

MediFamily functions identically across all 175 App Store countries and regions worldwide with zero geofencing, regional locks, or regional content variances.
