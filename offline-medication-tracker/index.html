Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Offline Tracking

Does MediFamily work completely without an internet connection?

Yes. MediFamily is built on a 100% on-device architecture. All medication schedules, inventory records, dose logs, and vital history are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Alarms, reminders, and inventory calculations function with zero cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.

How does the pill reminder wake my phone if it is in sleep mode?

MediFamily uses native operating system high-priority alarm schedulers. On iOS, it leverages local UserNotifications with time-sensitive authorization. On Android, it utilizes exact alarm permissions to wake the device from battery Doze mode with full lock-screen action buttons.

Can I track expiration dates on individual medicine boxes?

Yes. MediFamily features a multi-batch pharmacy inventory engine with First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) auto-deduction. You can enter separate batch lot numbers and factory expiration dates for each box. The app automatically consumes the earliest-expiring batch first and alerts you before stock runs low.

Is my medication data uploaded to any cloud servers?

Never. MediFamily enforces a zero-cloud, zero-knowledge architecture. Your records, medication names, dosages, and logs never touch external servers or third-party data brokers. Backups are exported as encrypted local archives directly under your personal control.

Can I purchase MediFamily without a monthly subscription?

Yes. While monthly and annual options exist, MediFamily provides a one-time Pro Lifetime license for $69.99 flat. It includes Apple Family Sharing at no extra cost, allowing up to five family members to share full Pro access for life.
