Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Family Management

How many family members can I track in MediFamily?

The free exploration tier supports up to 3 individual profiles. Upgrading to MediFamily Pro (available as a $69.99 one-time Lifetime purchase) unlocks unlimited family profiles, allowing you to manage kids, parents, and extended relatives in one app.

Does MediFamily include pediatric growth charts?

Yes. MediFamily includes interactive World Health Organization (WHO) pediatric growth percentile curves for height, weight, BMI, and head circumference from birth to 19 years, complete with interactive pinch-to-zoom and metric/imperial unit conversions.

Can I export health records to show my pediatrician or doctor?

Yes. With 1 tap, MediFamily generates a consolidated, printable PDF health summary for any selected profile. The report organizes active medications, vital reading trends, allergy lists, and immunization histories into a doctor-ready document.

How does Apple Family Sharing work with MediFamily Pro Lifetime?

Under Apple Family Sharing, a single MediFamily Pro Lifetime purchase ($69.99) can be shared across up to 5 family members on iOS without any additional fees. That equates to approximately $14 per person for lifetime ownership.

Is each family member's data kept private on the device?

Yes. All profile records, notes, photos, and vitals remain encrypted in the local SQLite database sandbox on your device. Zero data is uploaded to third-party cloud servers or shared with commercial brokers.
